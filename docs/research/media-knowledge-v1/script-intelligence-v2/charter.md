## Scope boundary

Included: ontology, metadata, original examples, research/evaluation protocols,
script-to-storyboard traceability and governance. Excluded until approval:
repository execution, model/data download, customer-data training, legal advice,
production publication and claims of measured audience impact.

## Missing-data scenarios

| Scenario | Assumption                                     | Consequence                                          | Confidence | Decision                  |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------------------------- |
| Base     | Charter approved; sources become accessible    | Populate one family at a time and pilot benchmark    | Medium     | 90-day pilot              |
| Downside | Sources/report remain unavailable              | Schema-only work; all substantive claims quarantined | High       | Do not score tools        |
| Upside   | Curated sources, reviewers and budget supplied | Parallel evidence packs and blinded pilot            | Low        | Board authorizes staffing |

## Acceptance criteria for Phase 0

Every dimension has an owner; unknown budget/deadline are resolved; audience and
platform assumptions are approved; prohibited scope and publication authority
are signed; evidence and privacy policies have named approvers.
